Frequently Asked Questions

01How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days, and orders over $200 ship free.

02Can I return a product?

Yes. Unopened items can be returned within 15 days for a full refund, and opened items can be exchanged within 30 days in their original condition.

03What do I need to create an account?

Just your name, an email address and a password. An account lets you track orders, save a wishlist and check out faster.

04Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and PayPal, plus direct bank transfer and cash on delivery.

05I forgot my password — what now?

Use “Lost your password?” on the sign-in page. In this demo store accounts live in your browser, so you can simply sign in again with any password of 8 or more characters.
